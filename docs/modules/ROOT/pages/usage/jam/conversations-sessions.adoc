[#turn-end]
== The turn-end rule

A turn ends in one of four ways:

* *Text and a destination:* `turn_end` with a `to` posts the final text there.
A `send`, `ask`, or `report` during the turn counts the same.
* *Text with no destination:* the turn is returned, below.
* *Text and no message needed:* `turn_end` with `+no_reply: true+`. The text stays as thinking.
* *No text:* nothing is posted, and the turn isn't returned.

Text is thinking unless it's sent.
The final text and mid-turn text are never auto-posted;
`turn_end` with a `to` posts the final text there.

A turn that ends with text, no destination, and no message sent during it is *returned* once.
`returned` is a per-turn flag, and the wake it causes has the reason `returned`:
Jam wakes the session at once (an explicit wake: wakes otherwise key off `WaitSeq`) with a note that the turn sent nothing and didn't say no message was needed,
and shows it the same messages again (they aren't committed).
If that turn also ends that way,
Jam commits, records `unaddressed` on the turn in the session view,
and doesn't return it again.

Interrupted, errored, and `/compact` turns are exempt, as today.
`turn_end` keeps `post: false`, read as `+no_reply: true+`.

[#session-view]
== The session view

The session view is read-only over the log and the Thinking feed:

* *Activity*: every message the session authored or was delivered, from any kind,
with its conversation.
`+GET /app/user/api/sessions/{id}/activity+`, paged like `read`.
* *Thinking*: the existing feed (xref:usage/jam/session-view.adoc[session-view]),
folded between messages a turn at a time, collapsed by default.
Thinking is never indexed for search.
* *Composer*: posts to `+session:<label>+` as the viewer, a `dm`.

*Who may open it:* the Jam's operators,
and for a personal session, the person who started it.
Everyone else gets `404`.
Roles have no owners;
if they get them, their owners join this list.
Thinking can quote what a tool printed,
so this is the transcript's access rule, not the conversation's.

After the session ends, the view stays, but without its header or pane.

[#escalation]
== Escalation

A tier opens a `dm` between the session and the tier's one person,
or a `group` of the session and the tier's people,
and posts the escalation notice there.
When the session works on a unit,
the notice is also posted to its log.
Tier timing doesn't change (xref:usage/jam/escalation.adoc[escalation]).

[#migration]
== Moving over

* Each live session channel is archived.
Its history stays readable in the session view.
* Each ticket channel becomes its unit's log and keeps its history, members, and binding to the issue.
Where a room is bound to the same issue, the binding moves to the log, and the room keeps its history.
* Each chat becomes a `dm` or a `group`.
* `+session:<label>+`, `+ticket:<key>+`, and `+chat:+` keep working as above,
so role files and context pages need no change at the switch.
* A personal session's starter gets a `dm` with it;
the idle ladder's nags go there.
* The Discord relay posts `dm` and `group` messages to people's inbox channels,
as it posts chats today.
