= The rail: sections and saved order
:summary: What `GET /app/user/api/rail` returns: the rail's sections (`me`, `work`, `agents`, `history`) and `unreadTotal`, and each person's saved order of `me` and `agents` (`PUT /app/user/api/rail/order`, the `rail_orders` table).
:read_when: You are building a client on the rail's sections or its saved order, changing the Jam app's rail (`web/app/src/rail`), or changing `userui.railSections`, `apiRailOrder`, `jam.RailOrderStore`, or the `rail_orders` table.
:owns: the rail response's sections and `unreadTotal`, how the Jam app shows them (the Me filter, reordering), the rail-order API, and its storage. Does NOT own the attention groups or the rest of the user area (intercom-ui.adoc), the status card (session-card.adoc), or who may open a session view (session-view.adoc).
:prereqs: intercom-ui.adoc for the rail and its groups; session-card.adoc for the card
:tier: leaf
:updated: 2026-11-15

`GET /app/user/api/rail` (`userui.RailResponse`) serves the rail two ways.
Its sections (below) are the rail the Jam app shows (<<app>>).
`groups` is the older attention grouping, which only the VS Code rail still shows;
`groups` goes once it reads the sections too.

[#sections]
== Sections

[%header,cols="1,4"]
|===
|Field |Holds

|`me`
|The live conversations the person is a member of, as `ChannelRow`s:
rooms, the work logs they follow, and the `dm`s and `group`s with a person or an active session besides them.
A `dm` with a session leaves when the session ends; archived conversations never show.

|`work`
|A `WorkRow` for every unit of work in the person's projects with a current worker, by key:
`key`, `title` (the issue title the Requisitioner raised the worker with, `Instance.UnitTitle`; empty for a manual raise), `log`, and `worker` (`id`, `label`, and its status card's `state`).
`unread` is present only when the person follows the log.

|`agents`
|An `AgentItem` (`session`, `ssh`, `card`) for every live, starting, paused, or recovering session whose view the person may open
(xref:usage/jam/session-view.adoc[session-view]).

|`history`
|The legacy log's conversations, read-only, newest first.

|`unreadTotal`
|The sum of `unread` over `me` and `history`: the app badge.
A push sets the badge to the same sum (`jam.UnreadTotal`, which counts the conversations `jam.InMe` keeps),
so the two never disagree.
A `groups` row outside `me`, such as a `dm` whose session ended, still shows its own count but isn't in the badge.
|===

`?c=` marks the open conversation `selected` and read in `me`, `history`, and `groups`,
and zeroes its `work` row's count.

[#app]
== The Jam app's rail

`web/app/src/rail/RailSections.tsx` shows the sections, in order:

* *Me:* one compact row per conversation:
its label;
for a `dm` with an active session, that session's status-card pill (such as *working* or *needs you*),
otherwise its kind (*DM*, *group*, *room*, *work*, or *agent*);
an orange dot when `needsYou` is set;
and an *unread* badge.
The *All*/*Unread* toggle in the heading filters on the client:
*Unread* keeps the rows with unread or `needsYou`.
* *Work:* the key and title, then the worker's label and state.
The row opens the work log;
the worker opens its session view (`/app/user/s/<session>`).
An *unread* badge shows when the row has `unread`.
* *Agents:* each session's xref:usage/jam/session-card.adoc[status card];
the card opens the session view.
* *History (before the upgrade):* collapsed under its heading at the bottom.

Work, Agents, and History show only when they have rows;
with none of the four, the rail says "No conversations yet."
The chimes (xref:usage/jam/push.adoc#sounds[push]) follow the `me` and `history` rows,
the ones `unreadTotal` counts.

A Me row or an Agents card is dragged by its grip (⋮⋮),
or moved one place with the grip's arrow keys.
The rail takes the new order at once (`useRailOrder`),
saves the section's whole order with `PUT api/rail/order`,
and refetches when the save settles,
so a refused save goes back to the saved order.
While *Unread* hides rows, a move goes to a shown neighbor's place,
and the hidden rows keep theirs.

[#order]
== The saved order

`me` and `agents` come in the person's saved order;
what isn't in it goes last, `me` newest message first and `agents` by start time.
`PUT /app/user/api/rail/order` with `+{"section": "me"|"agents", "ids": [...]}+` replaces the order (`204`; `400` for another section).
It keeps only the ids now in that section, once each, so ids that left it are dropped when the person next saves.
The order is stored per user in the store's `+rail_orders+` table (migration `0027`; `jam.RailOrderStore`),
so every client and device shares it.
It is kept when the person is removed, as their read cursors are.
