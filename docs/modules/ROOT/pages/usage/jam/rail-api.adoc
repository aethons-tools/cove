= The rail: sections and saved order
:summary: What `GET /app/user/api/rail` returns: the rail's sections (`me`, `work`, `agents`, `history`) and `unreadTotal`; each person's saved order of `me` and `agents` (`PUT /app/user/api/rail/order`, the `rail_orders` table); and the lookup of finished conversations and ended sessions (`GET /app/user/api/lookup`), with what a finished conversation allows.
:read_when: You are building a client on the rail's sections, its saved order, or its lookup, changing the Jam app's rail (`web/app/src/rail`), or changing `userui.railSections`, `apiRailOrder`, `apiLookup`, `jam.InMe`, `jam.Finished`, `jam.RailOrderStore`, or the `rail_orders` table.
:owns: the rail response's sections and `unreadTotal`, how the clients show them (`RailSections`: the `Me` filter, reordering), the rail-order API, and its storage; the lookup API, what makes a conversation finished (`jam.Finished`), and the `409` on posting to one. Does NOT own the rest of the user area (intercom-ui.adoc), the VS Code rail's clicks (vscode-hub.adoc), the status card (session-card.adoc), or who may open a session view (session-view.adoc).
:prereqs: intercom-ui.adoc for the user area; session-card.adoc for the card
:tier: leaf
:updated: 2026-11-18

`GET /app/user/api/rail` (`userui.RailResponse`) serves the rail.
Its sections are the rail the Jam app and the VS Code rail show (<<clients>>).

[#sections]
== Sections

[%header,cols="1,4"]
|===
|Field |Holds

|`me`
|The live conversations the person is a member of, as `ChannelRow`s:
rooms, the work logs they follow, and the `dm`s and `group`s with a person or an active session besides them.
A finished conversation (<<lookup>>) never shows:
a `dm` with a session leaves when the session ends, and a work log when its unit is done.

|`work`
|A `WorkRow` for every unit of work in the person's projects with a current worker, by key:
`key`, `title` (the issue title the Requisitioner raised the worker with, `Instance.UnitTitle`; empty for a manual raise), `log`, `worker` (`id`, `label`, and its status card's `state`),
and `state`, the unit's last reported state (xref:usage/jam/work-units.adoc[work-units]; empty when none).
`unread` is present only when the person follows the log.

|`agents`
|An `AgentItem` (`session`, `ssh`, `card`) for every live, starting, paused, or recovering session whose view the person may open
(xref:usage/jam/session-view.adoc[session-view]).

|`history`
|The legacy log's conversations, read-only, newest first.

|`unreadTotal`
|The sum of `unread` over `me` and `history`: the app badge.
A push sets the badge to the same sum (`jam.UnreadTotal`, which counts the conversations `jam.InMe` keeps),
so the two never disagree.
|===

`?c=` marks the open conversation `selected` and read in `me` and `history`,
and zeroes its `work` row's count.

[#clients]
== How the clients show it

`web/app/src/rail/RailSections.tsx` shows the sections in the Jam app and in the VS Code rail
(xref:usage/jam/vscode-hub.adoc#the-rail[vscode-hub], which owns that rail's clicks and ▣).
The caller makes each item open something.
In the Jam app, a `Me` or History row is a link to its conversation,
a Work row to its unit of work's page (`/app/user/w/<key>`, xref:usage/jam/work-units.adoc#page[work-units]), marked while it's open,
and a card and a Work row's worker are links to the session view (`/app/user/s/<session>`).

* *`Me`:* one compact row per conversation:
its label;
for a `dm` with an active session, that session's card state (such as *working* or *idle*),
otherwise its kind (*DM*, *group*, *room*, *work*, or *agent*);
an orange dot when `needsYou` is set;
and an *unread* badge.
The *All*/*Unread* toggle in the heading filters on the client;
*Unread* keeps the rows with unread or `needsYou`.
* *Work:* the key and title, then the worker's label and state,
and an *unread* badge when the row has `unread`.
* *Agents:* each session's xref:usage/jam/session-card.adoc[status card].
* *History (before the upgrade):* collapsed under its heading at the bottom.

Work, Agents, and History show only when they have rows;
with none of the four, the rail says "No conversations yet."
In the Jam app, the chimes (xref:usage/jam/push.adoc#sounds[push]) follow the `me` and `history` rows,
the ones `unreadTotal` counts.

A `Me` row or an Agents card is dragged onto another to take its place,
or moved one place with its grip's (⋮⋮) arrow keys.
The rail takes the new order at once (`useRailOrder`),
saves the section's whole order with `PUT api/rail/order`,
and refetches when the save settles;
a refused save shows a notification and goes back to the saved order.
While *Unread* hides rows, a move goes to a shown neighbor's place,
and the hidden rows keep theirs.

[#order]
== The saved order

`me` and `agents` come in the person's saved order;
what isn't in it goes last, `me` newest message first and `agents` by start time.
`PUT /app/user/api/rail/order` with `+{"section": "me"|"agents", "ids": [...]}+` replaces the order (`204`; `400` for another section).
It keeps only the ids now in that section, once each, so ids that left it are dropped when the person next saves.
The order is stored per user in the store's `+rail_orders+` table (migration `0027`; `jam.RailOrderStore`),
so every client and device shares it.
It is kept when the person is removed, as their read cursors are.

[#lookup]
== Looking up

The box at the top of the Jam app's rail looks up what the sections leave out:
conversations that finished and sessions that ended
(the manual's `inbox/channels.adoc`, *Looking up*).
The VS Code rail may follow on the same API.

`GET /app/user/api/lookup?q=<text>` (`userui.apiLookup`) returns a `LookupResponse`:

[source,json]
----
{"conversations": [{"row": ChannelRow, "title": "Fix the flaky login test",
                    "finished": "2026-11-12T16:02:00Z", "why": "done", "match": null}],
 "sessions":      [{"session": "ses_…", "label": "acme-42", "kind": "ticket",
                    "mission": "Fix the flaky login test", "ended": "2026-11-12T16:05:00Z",
                    "match": {"from": "acme-42", "text": "Ran the login test 200 times."}}]}
----

* *Finished conversations* are those `jam.Finished` names, that the person may read (`Intercom.CanSee`):
an archived channel (`why`: `archived`),
a `dm` or `group` with no person or active session besides them (`ended`),
and the log of a unit whose state is `done` (`done`).
The candidates are the person's conversations as the rail's
(members, or with deliveries to them) and the work logs of their projects.
A `group` has fixed members, so none is ever left.
`finished` is a done unit's report time, a `dm`'s or `group`'s last session's end,
or else (an archive keeps no time) the conversation's last message.
`title` is a work log's issue title.
* *Ended sessions* are the gone sessions whose view the person may open (`mayOpen`:
operators every one, a person their own personal sessions;
xref:usage/jam/session-view.adoc[session-view]).
`kind` is the session header's: `standing`, `personal`, `ticket`, or `session`.
`ended` is when it was last seen.
* `q` matches, case-insensitively, as the admin search does:
a conversation's label, a work log's ticket key and title,
a session's label and mission,
and the text of the messages in a conversation or a session's activity (the squawks it sent or was sent).
It never reads thinking, which is in the session events, not the log.
`match` is the newest message that matched, clipped to 160 characters around the match,
or `null` when the name matched.
An empty `q` returns two empty lists.
* Each list is newest first and holds at most 50.
Live conversations and active sessions are left out: the sections show them.
* Access is the Inbox's, checked per item before matching, never the admin console's:
an operator finds the conversations any person may read, plus every ended session.

A finished conversation opens at `/app/user/c/<channel>` as before, for anyone who may read it,
with `Conversation.finished` set to `why`, no `sendTo`, and so no composer.
A person's post to it (`POST api/messages`, `/app/user/send`; `ParticipantSendHandler`)
is `409` with `+code: "finished"+`, and nothing is sent.
An ended session opens at `/app/user/s/<session>`, its view keeping its activity.

In the Jam app (`web/app/src/rail/Lookup.tsx`), while the box has text
its results take the sections' place, in two groups,
*Finished conversations* and *Ended sessions*,
with *All*, *Conversations*, and *Sessions* to narrow them.
Each result shows its name and kind, a work log's title or a session's mission,
the matching message with the words highlighted, and why and when it finished.
Escape or ✕ clears the box and brings the sections back.
