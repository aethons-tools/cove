= Sessions and people talking — section index
:summary: Section index for the comms docs — the intercom tools and channel log, wake-on and turn end, the Jam app (`/app/user`), addressing, project members and rooms, push, Discord, whiteboards, attachments, asks and escalation.
:read_when: You are working on how sessions and people talk through Jam — an agent's intercom tools, waking and ending turns, the Jam app, who may address whom, notifications, Discord, whiteboards, files, asks or escalation — and need to know which doc to open.
:owns: the map of the comms docs
:prereqs: INDEX.adoc for the rest of the jam operator docs
:tier: section
:updated: 2026-11-13

Sessions and people talk in *channels* of Jam's channel log. An agent uses the xref:usage/jam/intercom.adoc[intercom] tools; a person uses the xref:usage/jam/intercom-ui.adoc[Jam app], Discord or the tracker. Open the doc for your task.

[%header]
|===
|Doc |Read when

|xref:usage/jam/intercom.adoc[intercom] |You want a raised studio's agent to read/send comments on its own ticket (the brokered intercom MCP), or you're wiring the `/squawks` endpoint + its `jam-studio-agent mcp` delivery, wake-on (`runtime.wake`), or running the intercom without a Requisitioner.
|xref:usage/jam/wake-on.adoc[wake-on] |You need to know why a waiting studio did or did not wake on a reply or was paused, you are tuning `runtime.wake`, or sessions stopped waking each other.
|xref:usage/jam/structured-turn-end.adoc[structured-turn-end.adoc] |You want to know what a woken agent is shown, why a session's reply was (or wasn't) posted or its messages committed, how an agent keeps a preview running between turns, or you're changing `+turn_end+`/`+keep_*+` or the turn-protocol switch.
|xref:usage/jam/turn-end.adoc[turn-end.adoc] |You want a ticket session to report its ticket's state or a session to end itself, to be woken at a time or on a schedule (optionally gated by a check), or to be woken (or torn down) after sitting idle; or you need to know why a studio shows `holding`, what a woken agent is told about why it woke, or how Jam decides a session whose turn ended may be woken, paused, or torn down.
|xref:usage/jam/intercom-ui.adoc[intercom-ui] |You want a project member to read/reply to their studios and channels in a browser — the `/app/user` Jam app (attention-grouped rail, conversations, New message, New session, asks, the composer, unread, live refresh, the session status strip) — or you're operating it (its URLs and old-link redirects).
|xref:usage/jam/intercom-composer.adoc[intercom-composer] |You are writing or changing how a person sends a message in the Jam app — Send, its keys, Plain text, the saved reply, the draft stack, or attaching a file.
|xref:usage/jam/session-card.adoc[session-card.adoc] |You are reading or changing a session's status card (rail row and extension Session Status pane) — its state and pill, counts, context bar, mission/focus, sparkline — or its data and live refresh.
|xref:usage/jam/session-colors.adoc[session-colors.adoc] |You want to know why a session or studio window has the color it has, or you are changing the session palette, its assignment, the `+session_colors+` table, or where the color shows (title bar, bubbles, composer, card icon).
|xref:usage/jam/session-view.adoc[session-view.adoc] |You're using or extending a session's conversation in `/app/user` — its header and actions, the Previews (View here)/Artifacts/Status/Thinking pane (what the agent thinks and does), its per-session layout memory, or who sees a session's cost.
|xref:usage/jam/push.adoc[push.adoc] |You want Jam to send push notifications to members' phones and desktops — `jam push keygen`, the VAPID credential, `runtime.push`, the push-service egress hosts — or a member asks about the Notifications menu item (missing, "Blocked", the iOS Home Screen rule), what buzzes them, lock-screen previews, the app badge, or sign-out on a shared device.
|xref:usage/jam/push-delivery.adoc[push-delivery.adoc] |You're debugging push delivery (`+push:relay+` open, `+push: send refused+`, a subscription vanished, a late or missing push) or extending the `+/app/user/push/*+` routes (key, subscriptions, focus) or the endpoint allowlist.
|xref:usage/jam/comms-addressing.adoc[comms-addressing] |You want a studio's agent to send to someone other than its own ticket (a named human or a channel), or you're granting/scoping who a studio may address, or managing a Project's members and rooms.
|xref:usage/jam/project-members.adoc[project-members] |You are adding or changing a project's members or rooms, linking a user's login, OIDC identity or tracker/Discord account, using `jam user|account|connection|room`, or a members or rooms change was refused.
|xref:usage/jam/discord.adoc[discord] |You are setting up a human's Discord inbox or binding them to their Discord user id (a member's `delivery` in the config repo, `+account add --connection discord --uid+`), setting a project's chat service, or a Discord reply was routed or attributed (to a member vs a display name) differently than you expected.
|xref:usage/jam/whiteboards.adoc[whiteboards.adoc] |You want people and agents to sketch together on a shared canvas in a conversation — board access, per-element merge, sync routes, the board_* agent tools, the server-side renderer, and the board editor in the Jam app.
|xref:usage/jam/attachments.adoc[attachments] |You want people or agents to share files (screenshots, logs, PDFs) in a conversation, you're setting up the attachments bucket (Garage/S3/R2), or an upload or download is refused — upload → reference → access-checked download, `attach`/`fetch`.
|xref:usage/jam/asks.adoc[asks] |You want an agent to ask people a question with answers to pick from (approve, choose A/B/C) and get the answer through `read` — the `ask`/`amend`/`withdraw`/`asks` tools, who may answer, and how open asks count as asking for a person.
|xref:usage/jam/escalation.adoc[escalation] |You want a waiting session that asked for a person to actively reach people — configuring a Project's ordered, category-keyed escalation tiers (called into the session's channel) + per-tier timeouts, the `escalate(category)` tool, and operating the resident escalation engine.
