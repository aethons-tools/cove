= A session's activity
:summary: The session view's activity — every message a session authored or was delivered, in every conversation (`GET /app/user/api/sessions/\{id}/activity`, paged like `read`), its turns of thinking folded between, Resume where the agent-to-agent limit is tripped, and the DM composer.
:read_when: You're using or extending the activity in a session's view: its route and paging, the thinking folds, Resume, or the composer that DMs the session.
:owns: `GET /app/user/api/sessions/\{id}/activity`, the channel log's activity reads (`ActivitySince`, `ActivityBefore`), the session-event store's turn spans (`Turns`), the thinking folds, the Resume notice, and the session view's composer
:prereqs: session-view.adoc for who may open a session view and its header and pane; comms-addressing.adoc for the `+session:+` target
:tier: leaf
:updated: 2026-11-14

The activity is the body of a xref:usage/jam/session-view.adoc[session view]
(`internal/jam/userui/api_activity.go`; the app's `web/app/src/session/ActivityList.tsx`, `TurnFold.tsx`, and `SessionPage.tsx`).
Who may read it is who may open the view
(xref:usage/jam/session-view.adoc#access[Who may open it]),
and it stays after the session ends.

`+GET /app/user/api/sessions/{id}/activity+` is every message the session authored or was delivered,
from every kind of conversation
(`ActivitySince` and `ActivityBefore` on the channel log, `intercom.Store`),
oldest first, each with its conversation (id, kind, and label)
and whether the session wrote it.
It pages like the session's `read`:
`anchor` is `end` (the default), `start`, or `id` (with `id`),
`dir` is `forward` (the default) or `backward` from an id,
and `limit` defaults to 50, at most 500.
A page carries its first and last message ids,
and whether messages exist before and after it.
Legacy squawks from before the cutover aren't part of it.

The app shows each message under its conversation's name, a link to the conversation,
and loads earlier pages with *Load earlier*.
It refetches on the events stream's `changed`, and every 30s.

== Thinking folds

A page also lists the session's turns whose thinking falls into it:
those starting from its first message up to the next page's first
(the oldest page from the beginning, the newest on to now),
across all the session's streams, at most 500
(`Turns` on `sessionevents.Store`: each turn's seqs, time span, and count).
The app places each turn between the messages by time,
as one collapsed line ("Thinking · turn 3 · 10:12:04 · 10 events").
Opening it fetches the turn's events from the Thinking page route
with `+after=+` and `+before=+` around the turn's seqs,
and shows them as narrative lines.
Thinking is never part of a search.

== Resume

A page names its conversations where the agent-to-agent limit is tripped
(`Deps.AgentLimit`, `Tripped(conversation)`; until it is wired, none are).
The app says so above the composer, with *Resume*,
which posts `+POST /app/user/api/conversations/{id}/resume+`.

== Composer

The page's composer sends to `+session:<id>+`, the dm between the viewer and the session
(xref:usage/jam/comms-addressing.adoc[comms-addressing]).
