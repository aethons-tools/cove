= The session view
:summary: The session view in the `/app/user` Jam app — one session's own page (its activity is session-activity.adoc), who may open it, and the header (identity, state, cost, context use, uptime, quick actions) and tabbed pane (Thinking, Previews, Artifacts, Status) it shares with the session's home channel; layout memory.
:read_when: You're using or extending a session's view in `/app/user` — who may open it, its header and actions (Copy SSH, Interrupt, Compact, Clear, Release, Restart, End, Upgrade), the side pane and its tabs, viewing a preview in the pane, the pane's layout memory and `?tab=` links, who sees the session's cost, or the Thinking tab (what the agent thinks and does: filters, formats, limits).
:owns: who may open a session view, the session view page (`/app/user/s/<id>`) and its layout, the session header (`GET /app/user/api/sessions/\{id}`) and its actions (`POST /app/user/api/sessions/\{id}/release|restart|upgrade`), the session controls (Interrupt, Compact, Clear), the session pane (`GET /app/user/api/sessions/\{id}/pane/\{tab}`) and its tabs, a preview's View here (`POST /app/user/api/sessions/\{id}/frame`), the Thinking tab (`GET /app/user/session/\{id}/thinking/events`, `GET /app/user/api/sessions/\{id}/thinking[/raw]`) and its options, the pane layout and its localStorage memory, and the cost/count aggregates (`sessionevents.Totals`) the header shows
:prereqs: intercom-ui.adoc for the user area this extends; session-events.adoc for the events the aggregates come from; previews.adoc for exposed ports; collaboration.adoc for who may SSH or open a preview; previews.adoc#framing-the-jam-pane for the frame tickets and cookie
:tier: leaf
:updated: 2026-11-14

A session's view is its own page in the xref:usage/jam/intercom-ui.adoc[Jam app], `/app/user/s/<id>`
(`web/app/src/session/SessionPage.tsx`): the session's header, its activity
(xref:usage/jam/session-activity.adoc[session-activity]), and a pane beside or below them.
The same header and pane also show over a live session's *home channel* (its session channel,
or the work log of the ticket it works), where the header's name links to the session view.
The API is in `internal/jam/userui` (`+api_sessions.go+`, `session.go`, `sessionpane.go`).

[#access]
== Who may open it

The Jam's operators may open any session's view, and the person who started a personal
session may open its view (`mayOpen` in `session.go`). Being in the session's home channel
grants nothing. Everyone else gets 404 from every route below, as for a session that doesn't exist. Once the session ends, its header, pane, actions, and live Thinking feed answer 404,
and the page keeps only the activity, saying the session has ended in place of the composer.

[#header]
== Header

One line, refreshed on push and every 30s (`+GET /app/user/api/sessions/{id}+`):

* *Identity:* the name (or actor id), kind (standing, personal, ticket, session), ticket,
role, project, and owner. Under them, the session's xref:usage/jam/intercom.adoc#what-the-tools-do[mission] (one truncated line), when it has one.
* *State:* the same status as the xref:usage/jam/intercom-ui.adoc#session-status[status strip], how
long it has been in that state, and open asks.
* *Cost:* the total and the latest turn's cost. The tooltip shows input and output tokens.
* *Context:* how full the agent's context is, e.g. ``+ctx 84k / 200k · 42%+``; the tooltip
gives exact tokens and the model. The size comes from the main thread's latest model call
(its input, cache and output tokens; subagents' calls don't count). The window comes from the
latest result's `modelUsage`. After a compaction, or a Clear or Compact press jam-studio-agent
carried out, it shows `+ctx —+` until the next model call.
* *Uptime:* time since the session was raised.
* *Tabs:* Thinking, Previews, Artifacts, Status. These open the pane.
* *Copy SSH:* copies `jam ssh <id>`. It appears only if xref:usage/jam/collaboration.adoc[collab] would allow you to SSH.
* *Open in VS Code* and *Open in VSCodium:* shown when Copy SSH is. Links to `+vscode://aethons.jam/session/<id>+` and `+vscodium://…+`, which the xref:usage/jam/vscode.adoc[Jam extension] handles. The session head also carries the session's `channel`, so the extension can link back to its page.
* *Interrupt:* a visible button beside Copy SSH, enabled while the session is running. See <<session-controls,Session controls>>.
* *⋯ menu:* Compact and Clear (see <<session-controls,Session controls>>; only Clear asks for confirmation), then the release, restart, end and upgrade actions, each of which asks for confirmation, then `+POST /app/user/api/sessions/{id}/<action>+`.
The server re-checks permission (403 otherwise). Once a session ends, its header and pane go.

[%header]
|===
|Action |Offered to |Does

|Release |a personal session's owner, or an operator |tears it down
|Restart |operators, every kind |restarts its studio, keeping the session: a standing one is raised again by its reconciler, any other is xref:usage/jam/coves.adoc#recovery[recovered] at once (re-raised with its state and conversation)
|End |operators, for non-personal sessions (a personal one ends by Release) |tears it down for good (its unfinished ticket is marked blocked)
|Upgrade |operators, standing sessions |queues an upgrade to the current image; the pending state shows beside it
|===

[#session-controls]
=== Session controls

Interrupt, Compact and Clear act on the session's agent; jam-studio-agent's handling is in
xref:usage/jam/studio-agent.adoc#session-controls-interrupt-compact-clear[studio-agent]. Anyone who may open the
session's view may press them, on any session kind. Clear asks for confirmation.

[%header]
|===
|Control |Enabled when |Does

|Interrupt |running |stops the current turn; the agent then acknowledges in its channel and waits
|Compact |running, holding (a turn ended with background tasks outstanding) or waiting |shrinks the conversation with Claude's `/compact`
|Clear |waiting, including paused |starts the next turn as a blank conversation, and resets the session's mission and focus, since Clear means a new task (a failed reset is logged and doesn't fail the Clear)
|===

Each press is `+POST /app/user/api/sessions/{id}/interrupt|compact|clear+`. The server answers 409
when the control isn't available in the session's state, and 502 when the cove can't be reached.
A paused studio is resumed first, so a press may take up to about 30s. The Thinking tab shows
each press as "<Command> — pressed by <name>", and a press jam-studio-agent dropped with its reason,
e.g. "Clear — pressed by Bob (ignored: live episode)".

[#pane]
== Pane

The pane is closed until a tab opens it. Clicking the open tab again, or *×*, closes it.
*⇄* moves it between the right of the messages and below them. Below 900px wide it always
sits below. Drag the divider to resize it, or focus the divider and use the arrow keys. The
composer stays under the messages.

Each tab is `+GET /app/user/api/sessions/{id}/pane/{tab}+`. The open tab reloads on push and
every 30s.

* *Previews:* the session's exposed ports (xref:usage/jam/previews.adoc[]). Each link opens in a
new tab. With framing on, *View here* shows one preview in the pane (see
<<view-here,View here>>). When previews are off, or collab doesn't allow you to open them,
the tab shows one message: "Previews aren't available to you here."
* *Artifacts:* what the home channel holds, newest first:
** whiteboards, with thumbnails;
** attached files;
** asks, with their state;
** links from message bodies, deduplicated, up to 50. GitHub PRs and issues are labelled
`+<repo> PR #n+` or `+<repo> issue #n+`.
* *Status:* state and time in it, uptime, model spec, kit and image, alarms, then from events:
turns and episodes, tool calls and errors, tokens, context use, cost (total and this turn),
and the last event.
* *Thinking:* the agent's events, live. See <<thinking,Thinking>>.

*Layout memory:* the open tab, placement and size are saved in `localStorage` under
`+jam-pane:<session id>+`. A session you have never opened starts from the last layout you
used (`+jam-pane:last+`). `?tab=previews|artifacts|status|thinking` on a session view or conversation URL
(`/app/user/s/<id>?tab=`, `/app/user/c/<channel>?tab=`) opens that tab. A remembered or linked `studio` tab (the
removed Studio, below) opens the pane closed. A page restored with the browser's Back or
Forward reloads a framed view.

== Studio (removed)

The pane's old *Studio* tab (code-server framed in the pane) gave way to the VS Code extension
(xref:usage/jam/vscode-checklist.adoc[]) over xref:usage/jam/collaboration.adoc[SSH]; a kit can still
`expose` a web IDE as an ordinary xref:usage/jam/previews.adoc[preview].

[#view-here]
=== View here

With framing on (previews are on and xref:usage/jam/serve.adoc[`ui-origins`] is set, since previews refuse
every other framer — xref:usage/jam/previews.adoc#framing-the-jam-pane[Framing (the Jam pane)]), each row in Previews
has *View here*. It replaces the list with that one preview framed, under a bar with
*← All previews*, the name, a status and *Open in new tab*. One preview is framed at a
time. Going back reloads the list.

* *Opening:* View here asks `+POST /app/user/api/sessions/{id}/frame+` (`+{"name"}+`, an exposed
preview's name, checked against the preview right) for a one-time frame URL, and loads it.
It reads "Loading…" until the frame loads.
* *Open in new tab* opens the preview on its own host, where you sign in normally. It works
even when a mint is refused.
* *Rate limit:* 30 mints per user per minute, then 429 ("Too many views opened; wait a minute.").

*Errors* show in place of "Loading…":

[%header]
|===
|Cause |Shown

|`signin` (the frame login lapsed) |re-mints once; if it recurs, a *Reopen this view* button
|`cookies` (an expired login, or the browser refused the frame cookie) |retries once, then "This browser can't sign in inside the pane — use Open in new tab." (Safari does this)
|403 (refused mint, or from the frame) |No longer shared with you.
|404, refused mint (not exposed, or framing off) |Nothing to show.
|404, from the frame |Nothing to show: the studio isn't running.
|a frame URL that isn't `http(s)`, or another refused mint |Couldn't open this view.
|502, 503, 504 |The preview isn't answering.
|===

== Cost and counts

`sessionevents.Totals` folds a session's events across all its streams into turns, episodes, tool calls, errors, tokens, and cost.
A turn's cost is its result's increase over the previous result of the same claude session (xref:usage/jam/session-events.adoc[why]); the cost is their sum.
`sessionevents.TotalsCache` keeps the totals per session and reads only newer events.

[#thinking]
== Thinking

The *Thinking* tab shows what the session's agent thinks and does, live, beside the chat.
Anyone who may open the session's view sees it (xref:usage/jam/session-events.adoc#sensitivity[Sensitivity]).
The server sends each event as `ThinkingEvent` JSON (`internal/jam/userui/thinking.go`, using `internal/jam/sessionview`);
the app (`web/app/src/session/thinking/`) filters and formats them in a virtualized list. Without recorded events the tab says so.

[%header]
|===
|Route |Returns

|`+GET /app/user/session/{id}/thinking/events+` |A live SSE stream of new events; 429 beyond 4 streams per viewer
|`+GET /app/user/api/sessions/{id}/thinking?stream=&before=[&after=]+` |An older page of 300 events (Load earlier), or with `after`, the events of one turn's fold
|`+GET /app/user/api/sessions/{id}/thinking/raw?stream=&seq=+` |One event's raw JSON, capped at 1 MB
|===

All three give 404 to anyone who may not open the session's view (as for every route above), and 204 when events aren't recorded; the page and raw routes also serve an ended session, for its activity's folds.
An open `events` stream re-checks access every 25s (its keep-alive) and ends once the viewer loses it.
When the stream is refused, the tab checks the pane: a 404 shows "This session is no longer available
to you"; anything else (such as the 429) retries after 2s, 5s, and 15s, then shows "Too many Thinking
views open — close one, or reload".

* *Filters:* Thinking, Text, Tools, Errors only, Progress. A tool dropdown narrows Tools to
one tool, and search matches each line's text, including its details.
* *Formats:* Narrative (one line each, the default), Detailed (full inputs and outputs),
Raw (the JSON). Every line shows its local time beneath it.
* *Lines:* 💭 thinking, 💬 text, → the prompt that started the turn (filtered with Text), ▶ a
tool call. Thinking with no visible text (only its signature came back) is a muted
"thinking" line, and a run of them collapses ("thinking ×3"). Anything else (system events
such as hooks, rate-limit notices) shows only its type, muted, with no detail.
* *Pairing:* a tool call and its result are joined, with ✓ or ✗ and the duration under the call's line, even when
Load earlier brings the call in after its result.
* *Dividers:* each result is a divider: its episode, its cost since the previous loaded result
of that episode (or, with none loaded before it, the episode's total so far), and its duration.
* *Following:* while scrolled to the bottom, new lines keep it there. Scrolling up pauses
following and shows "↓ N new". *Load earlier* fetches the previous page.
* *Streams:* the picker offers Current and past streams. A new stream (the studio
restarted) starts with a "session restarted" divider.
* *Memory:* filters, tool and format are saved per browser in
`+localStorage['jam-thinking']+`. The search isn't saved.

*Limits:* the view opens on the newest 300 events; page size is 300; a detail is clipped at
64 KB (the raw route reaches 1 MB); 4 streams per viewer; 2000 lines in the list, and
once older pages are loaded past that, following pauses.
