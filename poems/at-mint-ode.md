# An Ode to at-cove's Minted Credentials

Lo, at-cove now doth coin its own true keys,
A GitHub token, repo-scoped, minted fresh
For every git-step's brief necessities,
Then cast aside ere it corrupt the flesh.
And for the agent's mind, a bearer bright—
A Workload bond that lives but for the hour,
Whose short-lived breath doth grant it leave to write,
Then fades, and leaves no lingering trace of power.
No secret sleeps on disk, nor haunts the line;
Each credential blooms, then yields its fleeting shine.
