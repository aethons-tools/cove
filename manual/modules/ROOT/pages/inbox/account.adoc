= Your account, app tokens and notifications
:page-area: ROOT
:page-id: ROOT/inbox/account
:page-conventions: sentence-case

Your account menu is the round button with your initial
at the right of the title bar.
It shows your name and your role,
and the controls for this device and your connected apps.
There is no separate account page.

image::account-menu.png["The account menu",500]

The menu has these items:

* *Notifications*, for push notifications on this device.
* *Sounds…*, for the sounds Jam plays on this device.
* *Connected apps…*, for the apps that use Jam as you.
* *Sign out*.

The button left of it switches the color scheme:
light, dark, or the same as your system.

== Push notifications

Jam can send a notification to your phone or computer
when a message is for you,
even when Jam is closed.
You turn notifications on for each device and browser separately.

The menu item shows the state on this device:

Notifications: Off::
Click it to turn notifications on.
Your browser asks for permission first.
Notifications: On::
Click it to turn them off on this device.
Notifications: Blocked in browser settings::
You blocked notifications for Jam in this browser.
Allow them in the browser's site settings, then reload the page.
Add Jam to your Home Screen to get notifications::
On an iPhone or iPad, only the Jam app on the Home Screen gets notifications.
Add Jam to the Home Screen, open it from there, and turn notifications on.

If the item is not there,
this Jam does not send push notifications,
or this browser cannot get them.

Jam notifies you about:

* Each message in a chat or a session's channel that you are in.
* Each message in a room or a work log that names you, such as `@alice`.
* An ask that names you.
* A call-in that brings you into a channel.
* An escalation that calls you.

Jam does not notify you about your own messages,
or about the channel that you have open in a Jam window you are looking at.
A newer message replaces the channel's notification,
which then counts the new messages.
Click a notification to open its channel.
Where your device shows a number on the app's icon,
it is your count of unread messages.

The notification shows who wrote, the channel, and the start of the message,
also on the lock screen.
Your operator can hide the text.
Then it says "New message".

When you sign out,
Jam turns notifications off on this device first,
so that the next person on the device does not get yours.

== Sounds

*Sounds…* sets the sounds for this browser:

* *Messages*: for a new message in a channel you are in.
* *Needs you*: when a channel starts to need you,
for example for an ask, a call-in or an escalation.

Pick a sound for each, or *Off*.
Click ▶ to hear it.
*Volume* is for both.
Your changes save at once.

image::account-sounds.png["The Sounds dialog",500]

Jam plays these sounds while a Jam window is open,
also in a background tab.
It plays none for the channel you are looking at,
and at most one sound a second.
Your browser plays sound only after you click or type in the page once.

A push notification cannot play these sounds.
It plays your system's notification sound,
or none when that kind of sound is *Off*.
While a Jam window can play sounds,
Jam sends its notifications silently,
so you do not hear the same message twice.

== Connected apps

An app such as the Jam extension for VS Code
can use Jam as you, with an *app token*.
An app token is a key that Jam gives to one app.

To connect an app,
sign in from the app, for example with *Jam: Sign In* in VS Code.
Your browser opens a Jam page that asks if the app can use Jam as you.
Click *Allow* or *Cancel*.
The app can then see and do what you can in the Inbox,
and open SSH connections to the studios you can reach.
If you are an operator,
the page says that the app also gets your operator rights,
and the app can use the admin console's API.

*Connected apps…* lists your connected apps,
with when each was last used and when it expires.
An app with your operator rights shows *operator rights*.
Click *Revoke* to disconnect an app at once.

image::account-apps.png["Connected apps, with two VS Code tokens",500]

An app token lasts 30 days.
For a member, each use moves the end 30 days ahead.
A token with operator rights ends 30 days after you allowed it.
You can have 20 tokens at most.
A new one replaces the one you used least recently.

== Notes for the team

* Push needs a `runtime.push` block in the serve config, with the VAPID key as a credential
(`docs/modules/ROOT/pages/usage/jam/push.adoc`, "Enabling push on a Jam").
`preview: false` hides the text.
* Notifications go only to members of the channel who can see the message and have not read it.
A member keeps at most 20 subscribed devices.
* Focus: the app posts the open, focused channel to `POST /app/user/push/focus`; Jam forgets it after 60 seconds.
* Sounds are kept in the browser (`localStorage` `jam-sounds`, and IndexedDB for the service worker),
which silences a push while a page that can chime checked in during the last 90 seconds (`internal/jam/appui/sw.js`).
* App tokens: the consent page is `GET /app/auth/app-token` (`internal/jam/apptoken/consent.html`);
the dialog uses `GET` and `DELETE /app/user/api/app-tokens`.
An operator revokes a user's tokens on the user's admin page.
* The screenshots run in headless Chrome, which denies notifications, so the menu shows *Blocked in browser settings*.
* Architecture pages: `docs/modules/ROOT/pages/usage/jam/push.adoc`, `push-delivery.adoc`, `app-tokens.adoc`.
endif::[]
