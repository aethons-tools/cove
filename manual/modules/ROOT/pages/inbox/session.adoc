= Watching a session
:page-area: ROOT
:page-id: ROOT/inbox/session
:page-conventions: sentence-case

A *session view* shows one session:
everything it says and hears in every conversation,
and what it thinks between them.
It isn't a conversation.
Open it from the session's name anywhere in the Inbox,
or from its unit of work
(xref:concepts/work.adoc[Units of work]).
(A session is one run of an agent: xref:concepts/agent.adoc[Roles, agents and sessions].)

A *session header* at the top says who the session is,
what it does now, and what it costs.
Below it, the session's *activity* shows its messages in and out, in order.
A *pane* beside the activity shows what the session thinks and does,
its previews, what it shared, and its status.

image::session-view.png["A session view, with its header and the Thinking pane",800]

The session's owner and the Jam's operators can open its session view.
For a personal session, the owner is the person who started it;
for any other session, it's the owners of its role.

== Activity

The activity lists every message the session sent or got,
in DMs, group DMs, rooms, and work logs,
oldest first.
Each message shows which conversation it's in;
click that to open the conversation.
Between the messages, the session's thinking shows collapsed:
a line for each turn.
Click a line to open what the session thought in that turn.
Thinking isn't searchable,
and only the people who can open the session view see it.

The composer at the bottom sends a DM to the session.
You see your DM in the activity and in your Inbox.

When the session ends,
the view keeps its activity,
but its header and pane go.

== The header

image::session-header.png["The session header",800]

From left to right, the header shows:

Who it is::
The session's name and its kind:
*standing*, *personal*, *ticket* or *session*.
A ticket session also shows its ticket.
Under the name are its role, its project and, for a personal session, whose it is.
Under them is the session's mission, the goal it set for itself, if it has one.
State::
What the session does now, such as "is running *Bash*", "is idle" or "needs you",
and how long it has been in that state.
A number with *?* is its count of open asks.
Cost::
What the session has cost so far, and what its current or last turn cost.
Point at the cost to see the input and output tokens.
Context::
How full the agent's context is,
for example "ctx 84k / 200k · 42%".
Point at it to see the exact tokens and the model.
After the agent compacts or clears its conversation,
it shows "ctx —" until the agent next calls the model.
Uptime::
How long ago the session started, such as "up 1h 12m".

The header updates live, and at least every 30 seconds.

On the right are the pane's tabs
(<<the-pane,The pane>>)
and the session's actions.

=== Quick actions

*Copy SSH*::
Copies the command `jam ssh <session>`,
which opens a shell in the session's studio.
It shows only if the role or a share lets you connect to the studio
(xref:concepts/agent.adoc[Roles, agents and sessions]).
*Open in VS Code* and *Open in VSCodium*::
Open the session's studio in your editor.
They need the Jam extension,
and show only when *Copy SSH* does.
*Interrupt*::
Stops the agent's current turn.
The agent then says so in its channel and waits.
You can press it only while the session is running.

=== The ⋯ menu

The *⋯* button opens the other actions.
Some ask you to confirm first.
An action that you can't use now is greyed out.
If Jam refuses an action, a note says why.

[%header]
|===
|Action |Who sees it |What it does

|*Compact*
|Everyone who sees the session
|Shrinks the agent's conversation, so its context has room again.
You can use it while the session runs, holds background work, or waits.

|*Clear*
|Everyone who sees the session
|Starts the agent's next turn with an empty conversation.
The agent keeps its files and memory,
but forgets the conversation, its mission and its focus.
You can use it only while the session waits.
It asks you to confirm.

|*Release*
|The owner of a personal session, and operators
|Ends the personal session now.

|*Restart*
|Operators
|Restarts the session's studio and keeps the session and its work.

|*End*
|Operators, for sessions that are not personal
|Ends the session for good.
If it works on a ticket, the ticket is marked blocked.

|*Upgrade*
|Operators, for standing sessions
|Moves the standing session to the current studio image.
A pending upgrade shows beside the action.
|===

If the session's studio is paused,
*Interrupt*, *Compact* and *Clear* resume it first,
so they can take up to about 30 seconds.
The *Thinking* tab shows each press and who pressed it.

[#the-pane]
== The pane

The pane is closed until you click one of its tabs in the header:
*Thinking*, *Previews*, *Artifacts* or *Status*.
Click the open tab again, or *×*, to close it.
The open tab updates live, and at least every 30 seconds.

=== Layout

* *⇄* moves the pane between the right of the messages and below them.
In a window narrower than 900 pixels, the pane is always below.
* Drag the line between the messages and the pane to resize it.
You can also click the line and use the arrow keys.
* The composer stays under the messages.

image::session-below.png["The Status pane below the messages",600]

Jam remembers the open tab, the place and the size for each session, in this browser.
A session that you have not opened before starts with the last layout you used.

To link to a tab,
add `?tab=thinking`, `?tab=previews`, `?tab=artifacts` or `?tab=status`
to the conversation's address.
The tab then opens when someone follows the link.

=== Thinking

The *Thinking* tab shows what the agent thinks and does, live.
Each line has an icon and the time under it:

* *→* the message that started the turn;
* *💭* the agent's thinking;
* *💬* what the agent wrote;
* *▶* a tool call, such as *Bash* with its command.
Under it, *✓* or *✗* shows how the call ended and how long it took.
* A line across the list ends each episode, with its cost and its duration.

While the agent works, "working…" shows at the end.

image::session-thinking.png["The Thinking tab",400]

The bar at the top changes what you see:

* *Thinking*, *Text*, *Tools*, *Errors only* and *Progress* turn kinds of lines on and off.
* *All tools* narrows the tool calls to one tool.
* *Search* keeps only the lines that contain your text.
* *Narrative* shows one line for each event.
Click a line to see its details.
*Detailed* shows all the details,
and *Raw* shows each event as it was recorded.
* *Current* follows the newest run of the session.
After a restart, the session has a new run,
and you can pick an older one from the list.

The list stays at the newest line while you are at the bottom.
If you scroll up, it stays where you are,
and a "↓ N new" button counts the new lines.
Click it to go back to the bottom.
*Load earlier* at the top shows older events.

Jam remembers your choices in this browser,
except the search.

If your Jam does not record session events,
the tab says so.

=== Previews

An agent can serve a web app in its studio and _expose_ it as a *preview*,
a web address that people can open.
The *Previews* tab lists the session's previews,
each with its name and port.

image::session-previews.png["The Previews tab",400]

* Click a name to open the preview in a new browser tab.
You sign in there the first time.
* Click *View here* to show the preview inside the pane.
A bar above it has *← All previews*, which goes back to the list,
and *Open in new tab*.
The pane shows one preview at a time.

If the preview can't show in the pane, a note says why.
For example, some browsers, such as Safari, can't sign in inside the pane:
use *Open in new tab*.

If you aren't allowed to open the session's previews,
or your Jam has no previews,
the tab says "Previews aren't available to you here."

=== Artifacts

The *Artifacts* tab collects what the channel holds, newest first:

* *Whiteboards*, with a small picture of each.
Click one to open it (xref:inbox/whiteboards.adoc[Whiteboards]).
* *Files* attached to messages.
Images show small. Click a file to download it.
* *Asks*, each with its state: open, answered or withdrawn.
Click one to go to it in the conversation
(xref:inbox/asks.adoc[Answering asks]).
* *Links* from the messages, each one once, up to 50.
A GitHub pull request or issue shows as, for example, "app PR #61".

image::session-artifacts.png["The Artifacts tab",400]

=== Status

The *Status* tab lists facts about the session:
its state and how long it has been in it,
its uptime, model spec, kit and image,
and its alarms with their next time.
Once the agent has worked,
it also shows its turns and episodes,
its tool calls and errors,
its tokens, its context use,
its cost and the cost of this turn,
and when its last event was.
(xref:concepts/model-spec.adoc[Model specs], xref:concepts/kit.adoc[Kits and studios].)

== Session colors

Jam gives each session a color when the session starts,
and the session keeps it for good.
The color helps you tell sessions apart.
It never replaces the name.

The color shows:

* as a line on the left of the session's messages;
* on the left edge of the composer, in the session view;
* on the left edge of the session's status card in the rail;
* in the title bar of the session's studio window in VS Code.

Jam picks the color that is most different from the colors of the sessions that run now.
With more than 24 sessions, colors repeat.
A session started before Jam had colors has none.

* Goal: the session view replaces the session's own channel (COV-299).

(`SessionView`, `SessionHeader`, `PaneTabs`; Thinking in `thinking/`, View here in `frames/`).

* Routes: `+GET /app/user/api/sessions/{id}+` (the header),
`+POST …/sessions/{id}/interrupt|compact|clear|release|restart|end|upgrade+`,
`+GET …/sessions/{id}/pane/{tab}+`,
`+POST …/sessions/{id}/frame+` (View here, 30 a minute per user),
and the Thinking feed `+GET /app/user/session/{id}/thinking/events+` (SSE, 4 per viewer).
* The pane layout is in `localStorage` under `+jam-pane:<session>+` and `+jam-pane:last+`;
the Thinking choices under `jam-thinking`.
* *Interrupt*, *Compact* and *Clear* show only when `jam serve` wires the session actions (`hd.Ops`), which it always does.
* Colors: `internal/jam/sessioncolor.go`, the `session_colors` table.
* Architecture pages: `docs/modules/ROOT/pages/usage/jam/session-view.adoc`,
`session-colors.adoc`, `previews.adoc` and `session-card.adoc`.
* Screenshots: the `helper` session in `web/app/src/shots/mocks.ts`.
