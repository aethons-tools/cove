= Answering asks
:page-area: ROOT
:page-id: ROOT/inbox/asks
:page-conventions: sentence-case

An *ask* is a question from a session with answers to pick from.
The session posts it in a channel,
and a person answers it there.
The session does not stop and wait.
It can have several asks open at once,
and you can answer them in any order.

An ask stays open until a person answers it
or the session withdraws it.
While a session has an open ask,
its channel shows under *Needs you* in the rail
(xref:inbox/channels.adoc[Reading and replying]).
If nobody answers for a while,
Jam can call people in
(xref:concepts/escalation.adoc[Escalation]).

image::asks-conversation.png["A ticket channel with two open asks, one answered and one withdrawn",800]

== Open asks

The *Open asks* panel above the messages lists the channel's open asks,
with a count.
Click a question to jump to its card.
An ask that is for other people shows *for* and their names.

== The ask card

Each ask shows as a card where the session asked it.
The card shows the question
and, under it, one button for each answer.
A button can have a short line of detail under its label.

image::asks-card.png["An open ask: two answers, Other…, a comment box and Discuss",600]

To answer, click a button.
You can write a comment in the box first.
Jam sends your answer to the session in your name,
and the session wakes up to read it.

If none of the answers fits,
write a comment and click *Other…*.
*Other…* needs a comment.

The first answer wins.
If someone answered before you,
Jam says who and what they chose,
and your answer is not sent.

You can answer only if you are in the channel.
In a ticket's channel, any member of the project can answer.
A session can name the people the ask is for.
Then only they can answer,
and other people see the answers as grey tags they cannot click.
Sessions never answer asks.

== Discussing an ask

Click *Discuss* on an open card
to talk about the ask before you answer.
The composer shows *Replying about* and the question,
and starts your message with a "re:" line.
Click ✕ to stop.

image::asks-discuss.png["The composer, replying about an ask",600]

Your message is an ordinary message.
It shows a "re:" link above it
that goes back to the ask's card.
It does not answer the ask.

== Edited and closed asks

The session can reword an open ask.
The card then shows *edited*,
and a line in the channel shows the new wording.
If you answer while the session rewords the ask,
Jam refuses your answer and shows the new version,
so that nobody answers a question they did not see.

When an ask closes,
its card changes:

* An answered card shows ✓, who answered, the answer, and the comment.
A line in the channel says the same.
* A withdrawn card shows *withdrawn* and the session's reason, if it gave one.

When a session ends,
its open asks close as withdrawn.

ifdef::internal[]
== Notes for the team

* A session asks with its `ask` tool and rewords or withdraws with `amend` and `withdraw`.
Limits: 2 to 8 answers, question up to 2 KiB, comment up to 4 KiB.
* The app answers with `+POST /app/user/api/asks/{id}/answer+` (`+{version, choice, comment}+`).
A stale version is `409 ask_changed`; a closed ask is `409 ask_closed`.
* Cards: `web/app/src/asks` (`AskCard.tsx`, `OpenAsks.tsx`); the card's data is `userui.askCard` (`internal/jam/userui/asks.go`).
* The Discord and tracker relays skip asks: they are answered in the app only.
* Architecture page: `docs/modules/ROOT/pages/usage/jam/asks.adoc`.
* Screenshots: the `chn_acme42` conversation in `web/app/src/shots/mocks.ts`.
endif::[]
