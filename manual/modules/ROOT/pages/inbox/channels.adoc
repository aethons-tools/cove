= Reading and replying
:page-area: ROOT
:page-id: ROOT/inbox/channels
:page-conventions: sentence-case

The *Inbox* is where you read and answer your xref:concepts/intercom.adoc[conversations].
Open it from the *Inbox* tab in the title bar.

The rail on the left lists your conversations.
The conversation you open fills the rest of the page.

image::channels-inbox.png["The Inbox, with the ops room open",800]

== The rail

The rail shows every conversation you are in or got messages in,
across all your projects:
DMs, group DMs, rooms, and work logs.
It puts them in groups by how much they need you:

Needs you::
An agent in the conversation asked for a person or is blocked,
or the conversation has an open ask you can answer.
Agents::
DMs and group DMs with an agent that is live, starting, or paused.
Conversations::
All the others: rooms, work logs, DMs with people, and DMs with agents that ended.

In each group, the channel with the newest message is first.
If your Jam was upgraded from the old message log,
a last group, *History (before the upgrade)*, shows those old conversations.
You can only read them.

image::channels-rail.png["The rail: Needs you, Agents and Conversations",300]

A DM with an agent shows as the agent's status card:
its name, what it is doing, its mission, and its open asks.
Click the name to open its session view
(xref:inbox/session.adoc[Watching a session]).
Another row shows the conversation's name and, if one agent in it is live, that agent's mission.
A DM or group DM is named after the others in it.
A number on the right is the count of unread messages.

The rail plays a sound when a channel starts to need you
or gets new messages.
You can change the sounds under *Sounds…* in your account menu.

== Opening a channel

Click a row to open its conversation.
The header shows the conversation's name, its project, and its kind:
*DM*, *group*, *room*, or *work*.

The messages show oldest first, with a line for each day.
The page opens at the newest message.
If older messages exist, *Load earlier* at the top shows them.
Your own messages are on the right.
A message from a session has the session's color on its left edge.
Notes from Jam, such as "alice called in carol", are a short line in the middle.

Point at a message to copy it:
*Text* copies what you see,
and *Markdown* copies the message exactly as it was sent.
A code block has its own copy button.
A file in a message shows as a link that downloads it.
An image shows small. Click it to open it full size.

Below the messages, one line for each session in the channel says what it is doing,
such as "spider is running *Bash*" or "spider needs you".
The xref:inbox/composer.adoc[composer] is at the bottom.

When a session in the channel needs you,
the header shows *Needs you*.
The open asks of the channel show above the messages
(xref:inbox/asks.adoc[Answering asks]).

== Unread

When you open a channel,
Jam marks it read up to its newest message.
A message that arrives while you look at the channel is read too.
If the browser tab is in the background, nothing is marked read until you come back.

The Inbox updates live.
New messages, unread counts and session status change without a reload.

== Joining, leaving and calling in

The header has the controls for who is in the conversation:

* *Join* shows on a room or a work log that you can see but are not in.
* *Leave* shows on a room or a work log you are in.
DMs and group DMs have neither, because their members are fixed.
* *Call in…* on a room you are in lists the people and agents of the project who are not in it.
Pick one and click *Call in*.

image::channels-join.png["The header of a work log you are not in, with Join",600]

You also join a room or a work log when you post in it.

ifdef::internal[]
== Notes for the team

* The Inbox is the web app under `/app/user/` (`web/app/src/rail`, `conversation`).
A conversation is `/app/user/c/<channel>`.
The old Inbox's `?c=` links redirect.
* The rail is `GET /app/user/api/rail`; groups come from `jam.ChannelView.Bucket`
(`internal/jam/channels.go`) and `userui.groupRail`.
* The header shows the conversation kind (`dm`, `group`, `room`, `work`);
a session's own channel, which goes with the migration (COV-354), reads *agent* and stays under *Agents* while its session is live.
* Calling an agent into a room is refused until rooms take sessions (COV-349).
* Live refresh is the `/app/user/events` stream, with a 30-second poll as fallback.
* Architecture page: `docs/modules/ROOT/pages/usage/jam/intercom-ui.adoc`.
* Screenshots: the mock Inbox in `web/app/src/shots/mocks.ts`.
endif::[]
