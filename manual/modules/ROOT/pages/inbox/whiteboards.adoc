= Whiteboards
:page-area: ROOT
:page-id: ROOT/inbox/whiteboards
:page-conventions: sentence-case

A *whiteboard* is a shared drawing in one channel.
People and sessions use it to sketch together:
diagrams, plans and freehand notes.
Everyone who can read the channel can open its boards.
Everyone who can post in the channel can draw on them.

== Starting a board

In a channel you can post in,
click *New whiteboard* in the header.
Type a title and click *Start*.
Jam opens the new board,
and a card for it shows in the channel.

image::whiteboards-new.png["The New whiteboard dialog",500]

Sessions can start boards too.
Their boards show in the channel the same way.

== The board card

The card shows the board's title,
who started it and when,
and a small picture of the board.
The picture updates as the board changes.
Click the picture or *Open* to open the board.

image::whiteboards-card.png["A board's card, started by a session in a chat",400]

== Drawing

The board opens in the Inbox under a bar
with *← Back to chat* and the board's title.

image::whiteboards-editor.png["A board open for drawing",800]

The board is an Excalidraw canvas.
You can draw rectangles, diamonds, ellipses, arrows, lines, freehand strokes and text,
and move, resize, recolor and delete them.
You cannot add images.

Your drawing saves while you draw.
You do not click a save button.
If a save fails, Jam tries again.
If you leave the page with changes that are not saved yet,
your browser asks you first.

== Working live with sessions

The board is live.
When another person or a session changes it,
you see the change without a reload.
Two people can change different shapes at the same time.

A session reads the board as a list of shapes, labels and arrows,
and changes it with simple steps,
such as "add a box" or "connect these two shapes".
It can also look at a picture of the whole board.

Changes to a board do not post a message
and do not wake a session.
To get a session to look at a board,
say so in the channel.

If you can read a channel but not post in it,
its boards open with *view only*,
and you cannot change them.
The boards of an archived channel are also view only.

== Notes for the team

* The editor is `/app/user/boards/<id>` (`web/app/src/boards`), a lazily loaded Excalidraw chunk.
It saves the changed elements about 300 ms after the last change, at least every second while you drag.
Live changes come from the `board` event on `/app/user/events`, with a 30-second poll behind it.
* The card's picture is Jam's own render (`internal/jam/boardrender`), not Excalidraw's.
It has no hand-drawn style.
* The session tools are `board_create`, `boards`, `board_read`, `board_apply` and `board_snapshot`.
* Excalidraw's own font does not load in the screenshot server, so the editor screenshot shows a fallback font.
* Architecture page: `docs/modules/ROOT/pages/usage/jam/whiteboards.adoc`.
* Screenshots: the `chn_bobcoder` chat and board `brd_release` in `web/app/src/shots/mocks.ts`.
Its elements (`board-release.json`) and picture (`board-release-thumb.png`) were made once with `jam.ApplyBoardOps` and `boardrender.Render`.
endif::[]
