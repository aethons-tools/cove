= An agent's page
:page-area: ROOT
:page-id: ROOT/configuration/agents
:page-conventions: sentence-case

The *Agents* tab lists every xref:concepts/agent.adoc[agent] on the Jam,
running or not.
Click one to open its page:
its identity and grants,
its studio while it runs,
and what it has done.
The actions here work on any branch of the config repo.

== The Agents tab

image::agents-list.png[The Agents tab,800]

Each row shows the agent's name and id
(and its ticket, for a ticket agent),
its kind,
its project and role
(*+N* when it holds more grants),
its status,
whether its connector is set up,
whether its image is *stale*,
and when Jam last heard from it.
The links above the table show only the agents with one status:
*Running*, *Waiting*, *Setting up*, *Idled*,
or *Lost / terminating*.
The list refreshes every 3 seconds.

*Raise agent* raises a manual agent:
a label,
a role and project,
an optional unit (such as a ticket id),
and an optional first prompt.
Jam opens the new agent's page.
*Teardown* on a row stops that agent's studio,
after you confirm.
Both show only when Jam runs agents.

*Enroll agent* makes an identity for something Jam does not run,
such as a CI bot:
an id,
a role and project,
and optional destination overrides (`git=git-pat`).
Jam then shows its token once.
Copy it at that time:
Jam does not show it again.

== An agent's page

image::agent-page.png[A personal agent's page,800]

The header shows the agent's id,
its status,
its kind (with its owner, for a personal agent, and its name),
its project, role and ticket.
On the right:

* *Open live timeline* shows its session's events as they happen;
it shows when Jam records sessions.
* *Teardown* stops its studio and returns to the list.
* *Revoke* deletes its identity, so its token no longer works.

Each asks you to confirm.

An agent that is not running shows a grey note.
Its session and messages stay on its page as a record.

=== Identity

The agent's grants, as `project/role` tags.
Point at one to see the destinations it gives.
*×* removes a grant.
*+ Grant* adds one:
a role and project,
and optional destination overrides.
*Token expiry* shows when its identity ends,
from its role's TTL.

=== Runtime

When the studio was raised,
when Jam last heard from it,
which Jam process looks after it (*Lease*),
and where it runs (*Backend*).

=== Waiting and escalation

* *Waiting*: since when it waits.
A new message in its channel wakes it.
* *Escalation*: the open escalation,
which tier of people was called and when,
and on which chain
(xref:concepts/escalation.adoc[]).
* *Owner nags*: for a personal agent,
how many reminders it has sent its owner while it waits.
* *Inbox*: how far it has read its messages.

=== Egress

Which version of its role's egress list the studio runs with.
If Jam fails to apply a changed list,
*Re-apply* counts the failures;
after too many, Jam tears the studio down,
so it never runs under the wrong list.

=== Access

Who can reach the running studio, over SSH or a preview, and why:

* operators, always;
* the owner of a personal agent;
* the project's members, as the role allows
(*preview* and *ssh*, each `members` or `none`);
* each person it is shared with:
the rights, when the share ends, and who shared it.
*Unshare* removes a share and closes that person's SSH connections,
unless they have access in another way.

Below are the open connections
and the agent's *Previews*:
each web app it serves, as a link.
When the Jam has no SSH access or previews set up,
the card says so.
You share a studio with `jam studio share`.

=== Session, squawks and setup

*Session* lists the agent's recorded sessions.
Click one to open it on the timeline.

*Squawks* shows the newest 50 messages in its conversations;
*in Intercom* opens the full log of its messages.

*Setup*, at the end,
shows what the studio was set up with when you click it:
its kit, settings and image.
