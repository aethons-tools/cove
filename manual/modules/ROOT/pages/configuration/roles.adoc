= Configuring roles and raising agents
:page-area: ROOT
:page-id: ROOT/configuration/roles
:page-conventions: sentence-case

A project's xref:concepts/agent.adoc[roles] and agents are on its *Agents* page,
under *Config › Projects › _your project_ › Agents*.
Each role has its own page below it.
To change a role you must be on a branch of the config repo
(xref:configuration/config-branches.adoc[]);
each save is a commit to that branch.
Raising agents works on any branch.

== The Agents page

image::project-agents.png[The acme project's Agents page,800]

*Roles* lists the project's roles.
For each one it shows the destinations it may reach,
with the credential where the role maps its own
(`github → github-review`),
its TTL,
and its kit (*default* when it names none).
Click a role to open its page.

*Agents* lists every agent that runs in the project or holds a grant into it,
one row each:
its name and id (and its ticket, for a ticket agent),
its kind,
its roles here,
its status,
whether its image is *stale*
(older than what its role would run now),
and when Jam last heard from it.
Click an agent to open its page
(xref:configuration/agents.adoc[]).
The page refreshes every 3 seconds.

On a branch, *New role* opens a short form:
a name,
destinations (comma-separated, `name=credential` to map a credential),
a TTL in seconds,
a kit,
and a model spec (blank means `claude-default`).
*Create role* makes the role and opens its page.

== A role's page

image::role-page.png[The reviewer role's page,800]

The top line shows the role's project,
kit, model spec and TTL.
The sections below show the whole role.
On a branch, each section has its own *Edit …* panel,
already filled in,
that saves only that section.

Scope::
Each destination,
the credential Jam adds for it
(*role mapping*, or the *destination default*),
and its route.
A destination that does not exist is marked *unregistered*.
*Addressing* lists who the role's agents may message;
with none, they cannot message anyone.
*Edit scope* changes the destinations, addressing, TTL (such as `1h`), kit and model spec.
Egress::
The outside domains the role's studios may reach,
within what the kit allows,
or *Kit default* when the role sets none.
In *Edit egress*, put one domain per line;
a leading dot (`.golang.org`) also allows its subdomains.
Saving an empty list allows nothing extra.
*Reset to kit default* removes the role's list.
Running agents get the change within a short while.
Allocation::
How many agents the role may have at once,
and the reminders for idle personal agents.
A value that is not set shows what applies instead.
The role's idle timeout, what happens then, and its alarm time zone show here too,
but you cannot change them on this page.
Agent context::
The instructions every agent of the role is given,
edited like a project's
(xref:projects:agent-context.adoc[]),
without resources.
Standing agents::
Each standing agent's name, prompt and status.
Holders::
Every agent that holds the role.
*override* marks a grant that changes the role's destinations.
Open an agent to manage its grants.
Running::
The role's running agents.
Setup::
Click it to see what a studio of this role would get if it were raised now.

*Operator notes*, above the sections,
hold notes for operators only;
agents never see them.
They show when there are some, and on a branch,
where *Add notes* or *Edit* changes them.
On a branch, *Delete* removes the role and returns to the Agents page.

=== Allocation

image::role-allocation-edit.png[Editing the reviewer role's allocation,800]

*Max ephemeral*::
The limit on ticket and manual agents running at once.
*Max personal*::
The limit on personal agents, for all owners together.
When it is not set, the role has no personal agents.
*Max personal per owner*::
The limit for one person.
*Idle after*, *Nag every*::
When a waiting personal agent first reminds its owner (default 4h),
and how often after that (default 24h).
*Reclaim after*::
When Jam ends a personal agent that still waits (default never).

Times take values such as `30m`, `1h30m` or `72h`.
Leave a field blank to unset it.

== Standing agents

On a branch, *Declare standing agent* takes a name and a prompt.
Jam raises the agent once you apply the branch,
and keeps it running.

Each standing agent has these actions:

*Upgrade*::
Restarts it on the current image,
once it is between turns.
It keeps its conversation and files.
When it is marked *image stale*, the button is highlighted;
while the upgrade waits, an *upgrade:* tag shows how far it has got.
*Reset*::
Deletes its conversation and files.
Jam raises it fresh, as a new session.
*Dismiss* (on a branch)::
Stops it and deletes its conversation and files.

Each action asks you to confirm.
*Upgrade* and *Reset* work on any branch.

== Raising agents

=== Requesting a personal agent

*Request* on a role's row,
or *Request agent* on its page,
raises a personal agent of that role for you.
It starts with the prompt
"Squawk me (user:_you_) and we will get to work.",
so it opens a conversation with you in the Inbox.
A message says that it was raised,
or why it was not.

You must be signed in,
and your login must belong to a member of the project.
The role's limits apply:
*Max personal* must be set,
and neither it nor your share may be used up.
Members can also start one from the Inbox with *New session*.

=== Raising and tearing down from the Agents tab

The *Agents* tab (xref:configuration/agents.adoc[]) has *Raise agent*:
a label,
a role and project,
an optional unit (such as a ticket id),
and an optional first prompt.
Each running agent there has *Teardown*.

Raising, requesting, upgrading and tearing down need Jam to run agents.
On a Jam that runs none, those buttons do not show.
