= About this manual
:page-area: about
:page-id: about

This manual describes the Jam app the way you use it:
what each page is for,
what you can do there,
and what happens when you do.

It comes in two versions.
*As is* describes the product as it works today.
*Goal* describes the product we are steering toward.
Where the two differ, that difference is the work still to do.

Pages are grouped by area.
The *Conventions* area describes choices that hold across the whole app,
such as how labels are written.
