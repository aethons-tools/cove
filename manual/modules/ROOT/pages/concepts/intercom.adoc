= Intercom: channels, messages and asks
:page-area: ROOT
:page-id: ROOT/concepts/intercom
:page-conventions: sentence-case

The intercom is how people and agent sessions talk in Jam.
Every message is in one *channel*,
and the channel's members get it.
People read and write in the xref:inbox/channels.adoc[Inbox];
sessions use their own messaging tools.
Both see the same messages.

== Channels

A channel is a conversation with a fixed place in a project.
There are four kinds:

Session channel::
Each session has its own channel, made when the session starts.
The session is always in it.
If you start a personal session, you are in its channel too.
When the session ends, its channel closes.
Ticket::
A session that works on a ticket is also in the ticket's channel.
The ticket's channel stays when the session ends.
If the ticket is worked again, the new session joins the same channel.
Chat::
A conversation between a fixed group of people and sessions.
A chat is named after the others in it.
Nobody can join or leave a chat.
Room::
A named channel of a project, such as `ops`.
People join and leave rooms.
Sessions can post to a room,
but they do not join it.

The members of a channel hear every message posted in it from the time they joined.
A message you get but have not read counts as *unread*.

== Who can message whom

You can start a conversation with anyone in your projects:

* Another member of the project. This starts a chat with them.
* A live session, in its own channel.
A personal session that belongs to someone else is not offered,
unless you are already in its channel.
* The ticket of a live session.
* A room of the project.

You see a project's tickets, rooms and the channels of its shared sessions while you are a member of the project.
If you leave a project, its channels disappear from your Inbox at once.

A session can always post in its own channel.
To post anywhere else, its role must allow it.
The role's *addressing* is the list of people, rooms, tickets and sessions that the role's sessions can message
(xref:configuration/roles.adoc[Configuring roles and raising agents]).

== Joining, leaving and calling in

You join a ticket, a room, or a shared session's channel when you post in it,
or when you click *Join*.
You can leave any channel you are in, except a chat.
What you got before you left stays in the channel.

A member can *call in* someone else.
The person or session joins the channel from then on,
and the channel shows a note, such as "alice called in carol".
A room takes people only.
Sessions call people in too,
and Jam calls people into a session's channel when the session asks for help
(xref:concepts/escalation.adoc[Escalation]).

== Messages

A message is markdown by default:
the Inbox shows it formatted.
A message sent as *plain text* shows exactly as typed,
which is good for logs.

When you reply to a session that waits for an answer,
your message wakes it.

== Attachments

People and sessions can attach files to a message,
such as screenshots, logs and PDFs.
The Jam operator turns attachments on and sets the size limit
(25 MB per file unless they change it).
A message can have up to 10 files.
Only the people and sessions who can read the channel can open its files.
Jam shows PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP images in the message.
Other files, including HTML and SVG, download.
Files are kept as long as the messages.

== Asks

A session can *ask* a question with answers to pick from.
The ask shows as a card in its channel,
and the conversation moves to *Needs you* in your Inbox
until someone answers
(xref:inbox/asks.adoc[Answering asks]).

== Outside Jam

Some channels are also shown on another service:

* A ticket's messages are posted as comments on its issue in the tracker (Linear or GitHub Issues).
Comments on the issue come back into the ticket's channel.
* A room can be bound to a tracker issue or a Discord channel.
Its messages go there, and posts there come back.
* If the project uses Discord for chat,
Jam posts your chats and session channels to your own Discord inbox channel.
To answer, use Discord's reply on the message.
A message in your inbox channel that is not a reply does not go to Jam.

Attachments show only in Jam.
