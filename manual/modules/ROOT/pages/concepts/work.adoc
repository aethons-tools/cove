= Units of work
:page-area: ROOT
:page-id: ROOT/concepts/work
:page-conventions: sentence-case

A *unit of work* is one ticket from the project's tracker,
seen from Jam:
the issue,
the agents that worked or work on it,
the pull requests they opened,
its state,
and its *work log*.
It isn't a conversation.
It outlives the agents that work on it.

Jam starts a ticket agent when a ticket is ready and marked for dispatch
(xref:concepts/agent.adoc[Roles, agents and sessions]).
If the ticket is worked again later,
the new agent takes over the same unit of work,
and reads its log as the history of the work.

== The work log

The work log is the record of the work:

* The agent's progress reports and its state changes, such as *in review*.
* Messages that people and agents post to the work.
* Asks about the work, and their answers.
* Review outcomes and decisions.

The whole log is mirrored to the issue in the tracker, both ways.
Each entry becomes a comment on the issue,
and a comment written on the issue becomes an entry in the log.

== Reaching the work

There are two ways to reach a unit of work,
for two different purposes.
Choose the one that fits:

Post to the work log::
For anything that matters to the work:
a decision, a change to what is wanted, an answer to keep.
The entry stays with the work,
shows on the issue,
and reaches the agent working on it now.
A later agent reads it too.
DM the worker::
For a conversation with the agent working on it now:
a quick question, or a nudge about this run.
Jam sends your message as a DM to that agent.
If no agent is working on the unit of work,
Jam doesn't send the message and says so.
The DM stays with that agent, not with the work.

== Seeing the work

A unit of work's page shows its issue,
its state,
its agents and their session views,
its pull requests,
and its log.
