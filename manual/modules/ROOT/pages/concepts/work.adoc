= Units of work
:page-area: ROOT
:page-id: ROOT/concepts/work
:page-conventions: sentence-case

A *unit of work* is one ticket from the project's tracker,
seen from Jam:
the issue,
the session that works on it now,
the sessions that worked on it before,
the pull requests they reported,
and its *work log*.
It outlives the sessions that work on it.

Jam starts a ticket agent when a ticket is ready and marked for dispatch
(xref:concepts/agent.adoc[Roles, agents and sessions]).
If the ticket is worked again later,
the new session takes over the same unit of work.
At most one session works on a unit at a time.

Jam remembers each session that has worked on the unit,
and when it started and stopped.
When a session reports a pull request for the ticket,
Jam keeps it with the unit too.

== The work log

The work log is the conversation of the work
(xref:concepts/intercom.adoc[Intercom]).
The session working on the unit gets every entry.
So do the people who follow it:
who posted in the log, or clicked *Join* on it.
When a new session takes over the unit,
Jam takes the old session out of the log and puts the new one in.
The new session reads the log from its first entry,
so it takes up the history of the work:
earlier sessions' reports,
and what people decided and answered.
Only the session working on the unit now can read the log this way.

The log holds the record of the work:

* The session's progress reports and its state changes, such as *in review*.
* Messages that people and sessions post to the work.
* Review outcomes of the unit's pull requests:
each review, and the merge or the close,
once each,
saying who reviewed it, the outcome, and a link to the review.

The log is mirrored to the issue in the tracker, both ways.
Each entry becomes a comment on the issue,
and a comment written on the issue becomes an entry in the log.
When the session reports its progress or a change of state, such as *in review*,
the report is an entry in the log,
so it shows on the issue as a comment.

== Reaching the work

Agents and people reach a unit of work in two ways,
for two different purposes.
In the Inbox, people pick *Log* or *Worker* on the unit in *New message*
(xref:inbox/composer.adoc#_starting_a_conversation[Starting a conversation]):

Post to the work log::
For anything that matters to the work:
a decision, a change to what is wanted, an answer to keep.
The entry stays with the work,
shows on the issue,
and reaches the session working on it now.
DM the worker::
For a conversation with the session working on it now:
a quick question, or a nudge about this run.
Jam sends the message as a DM to that session.
If no session is working on the unit of work,
Jam doesn't send the message and says so.
The DM stays with that session, not with the work.

An agent can always post to the log of its own ticket.
To reach other work, its role's addressing must allow the ticket
(xref:configuration/roles.adoc[Configuring roles and raising agents]).

== Seeing the work

A unit of work has its own page in the Inbox.
To open it,
click its row under *Work* in the rail,
or the ticket in the header of its agent's session view.

image::work-page.png["A unit of work's page: the work log, with its agents and pull requests beside it",800]

The page has three parts:

The header::
The ticket's key and title,
which link to the issue in the tracker,
and the unit's state,
such as *in review*.
The log::
The work log fills the middle of the page,
with a box under it to post to the log.
The panel::
The panel beside the log lists the unit's agents and its pull requests.
The agent working on it now comes first, with its status,
then the agents that worked on it before,
with the dates they worked when Jam knows them.
Click an agent to open its session view,
if you may open it.
Each pull request shows its number, its title,
and whether it's open, merged, or closed,
and links to it.
Click *Hide* to fold the panel away.

On a narrow screen,
the log and the panel are two tabs.

If you may not read the unit's log,
the page says it isn't found.

== Notes for the team

* The targets are `work:<key>` (the log; `ticket:<key>` is an alias) and `worker:<key>` (the DM;
`409 {"error": "no-worker"}` with no current worker).
Both match a role's `ticket:` addressing globs.
* The log is a channel of kind `work`; it records its current worker.
* The current worker reads the log's history with the intercom's `read` and `channel: work:<key>`;
the ticket brief tells it so.
See `work-log-history.adoc`.
* People post to a unit's log with *Log* in the Inbox's *New message*,
and DM its worker with *Worker*:
a recipient's `worker` field (`worker:<key>`), offered while the unit has a live worker they may reach.
From the Inbox, no worker is `409` with `code: "no-worker"`.
* A unit of work's page is `/app/user/w/<key>` in the web app (COV-408),
built on `+GET /app/user/api/work/{key}+` (COV-405);
the current worker's status comes from the rail's `WorkWorker.state`, since `UnitWorker` has none.
See `work-units.adoc#page`.
In VS Code, a Work row still opens the work log.
* Architecture pages: `docs/modules/ROOT/pages/usage/jam/comms-addressing.adoc` (targets and addressing),
`intercom.adoc` (the channel log), `work-units.adoc` (the unit's record, its API, and its page).
endif::[]
