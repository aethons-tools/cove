= Units of work
:page-area: ROOT
:page-id: ROOT/concepts/work
:page-conventions: sentence-case

A *unit of work* is one ticket from the project's tracker,
seen from Jam:
the issue,
the session that works on it now,
and its *work log*.
It outlives the sessions that work on it.

Jam starts a ticket agent when a ticket is ready and marked for dispatch
(xref:concepts/agent.adoc[Roles, agents and sessions]).
If the ticket is worked again later,
the new session takes over the same unit of work.
At most one session works on a unit at a time.

== The work log

The work log is the conversation of the work
(xref:concepts/intercom.adoc[Intercom]).
The session working on the unit gets every entry.
So do the people who follow it:
who posted in the log, or clicked *Join* on it.
When a new session takes over the unit,
Jam takes the old session out of the log and puts the new one in.

The log is mirrored to the issue in the tracker, both ways.
Each entry becomes a comment on the issue,
and a comment written on the issue becomes an entry in the log.

== Reaching the work

An agent reaches a unit of work in two ways,
for two different purposes:

Post to the work log::
For anything that matters to the work:
a decision, a change to what is wanted, an answer to keep.
The entry stays with the work,
shows on the issue,
and reaches the session working on it now.
DM the worker::
For a conversation with the session working on it now:
a quick question, or a nudge about this run.
Jam sends the message as a DM to that session.
If no session is working on the unit of work,
Jam doesn't send the message and says so.
The DM stays with that session, not with the work.

An agent can always post to the log of its own ticket.
To reach other work, its role's addressing must allow the ticket
(xref:configuration/roles.adoc[Configuring roles and raising agents]).

ifdef::internal[]
== Notes for the team

* The targets are `work:<key>` (the log; `ticket:<key>` is an alias) and `worker:<key>` (the DM;
`409 {"error": "no-worker"}` with no current worker).
Both match a role's `ticket:` addressing globs.
* The log is a channel of kind `work`; it records its current worker.
* People can't DM a unit's worker from the Inbox yet,
and a unit of work has no page of its own yet (COV-308).
* Architecture pages: `docs/modules/ROOT/pages/usage/jam/comms-addressing.adoc` (targets and addressing),
`intercom.adoc` (the channel log).
endif::[]
