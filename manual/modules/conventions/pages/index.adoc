= How the Jam app behaves
:page-area: conventions
:page-id: conventions

These choices hold across the whole Jam app.
Each page lists the conventions it follows in `:page-conventions:`,
so a change to a convention shows which pages it touches.

[#sentence-case]
== Labels are in sentence case

Headings, labels and buttons capitalize only their first word and proper nouns:
_Agent context_, _Save context_, _Chat service_, _Jam_, _Linear_.

[#quiet-grid]
== Short lists are quiet grids

A short list you edit,
such as a project's resources,
looks like a plain table.
A cell becomes a field only while you edit it:
click it,
press Tab or Enter to move on,
Esc to leave.
A choice from a fixed set shows its ▾ when you point at it.

[#collapsible-rows]
== Long items are collapsible rows

An item with long text,
such as a progressive instruction,
is a row that shows a summary.
▸ expands it in place to its fields;
▾ collapses it.
Never a dialog or a tray.

[#add-at-bottom]
== Add from the bottom of the list

You add an item from the *+ new …* row at the bottom of its list.
There is no add button at the top.
The row disappears when the list is full.

[#thermometers]
== Limits show as thermometers

Every field with a length limit has a thermometer below it,
with the count against the limit.
It turns amber near the limit and red over it.
A limit shared by a whole list has one thermometer below the list.

[#view-then-edit]
== One layout for viewing and editing

A card looks the same whether you are reading or editing it.
It is read-only until you click *Edit*;
then the whole card is editable,
and *Cancel* or *Save* returns it to read-only.
A card has one *Save*.

[#field-errors]
== Errors show at the field

A problem shows on the field that has it,
the footer counts problems,
and *Save* stays off until there are none.

[#rendered-markdown]
== Markdown shows formatted

Markdown is shown formatted while you read
and as source while you edit.
